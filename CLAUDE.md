# crucibleinnovation.com

Marketing site for Crucible (studio, `/`) and Crucible Sites (websites for local businesses, `/sites`, the page the business-card QR opens). Owner: Jacob Schopp. Read `README.md` for setup, deploy and the launch TODO list.

## Stack and commands
- Next.js 15 App Router, **static export** (`output: 'export'` → `out/`), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4. No server: no API routes, no `next/headers`, no runtime redirects, no image optimizer.
- `npm run dev` · `npm run build` · `npm run typecheck` · `npm test` (Playwright smoke tests against `out/`, so build first; locally set `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH` if browsers live elsewhere, or `PW_CHROMIUM_PATH` to a Chromium binary).
- Deploy: push to `main` → `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` builds and publishes to GitHub Pages (custom domain via `CNAME`). PRs run `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (typecheck, build, Playwright, Lighthouse CI with `lighthouserc.json`).
- Repo variables (optional): `UMAMI_WEBSITE_ID` (analytics), `FORMSPREE_ID` (in-page form posting; without it the form composes an email via mail app/Gmail/Outlook).

- The liquid (`lib/liquid/`, mounted by `components/Liquid.tsx`): the whole site is a dark liquid. The surface is a damped wave-equation grid on the main thread (`sim.ts`: 8 px cells, 120 Hz substeps, `SIM.damp` 1.2/s, 420 px/s, anchored to the page, sponge edges, stepped only inside a box that grows faster than its waves, no cost while quiet) for taps and floaters, plus the pointer's wake and an analytic idle swell. The wake (`wake.ts`) is a Kelvin-like model of the pointer's recent path, because a non-dispersive grid can only make rings around a slow source: samples every 10-28 px of path, each carrying two arms that leave the path sideways at 0.33x the pointer's speed (a ~19° V at any speed), broadening and fading over ~3 s, faint transverse ridges at medium speeds, spread by length-weighted Gaussians so a straight or curved path is one continuous surface (no beads, no rings); it is evaluated into the sim's display grid after each step, so the shader and every floater (`samplePx`/`footprint`, the logo, the ingots) read the same heights. `input.ts`: hover makes the wake, a drag (mouse button held, finger on the glass) 1.85x; a resting cursor the page scrolls past leaves the same wake along its page path; a finger's wake lives in viewport px (a pan leaves a trail on the glass) and after lift the spot it let go of rides on with the page through momentum; a tap is one clean drop; floaters shed small drops (`addSource(..., {kind: 'float'})` in `field.ts`). The idle ocean (`swell.ts`: `swellAt` on the CPU, `swellGLSL` in shaders, the same sum) is 8 long-crested waves travelling down-right with deep-water dispersion (`molten.ts` uses the first 4 as `uSwell`). `shader.ts` shades it, never outlines it: a dark sky reflection, a warm Blinn-Phong key that is a horizontal strip of light, not a round lamp (`LOOK.coreX`), with crisp Ember glints on interaction waves only that fade in with curvature (`LOOK.crisp`: streaks along crests, no puddles), a crest light on interaction waves from any direction (`LOOK.crest`, so a wake reads whichever way it runs), a soft warm sheen on the swell's crests (`LOOK.sheen`), no wave ever shades darker than the swell's own sheen (`LOOK.dip`; `LOOK.shade` 0.15 only for faces tilted away from the key), a faint caustic; no contour lines. There are no masks anywhere (Jacob: waves pass under text, portraits and every figure; a dimmed box behind content read as a black outline); glints are thin and pass in a moment. `render.ts` posts each frame's heights to `liquidWorker.ts` (OffscreenCanvas); where the worker can't get WebGL2 (Playwright WebKit, older Safari) the same `engine.ts` draws on the main thread. `engine.ts` holds the quality steps (render scale, fps cap; on a real GPU never below 1 canvas px per CSS px and no fps cap, since low steps magnified the glints into blocks on iPhone) and detects software GL (SwiftShader/llvmpipe: CI, PageSpeed), which starts low and never gives up; `?liquid=full` pins the top step (screenshots). It starts at the visitor's first scroll, pointer move or key (`firstUse()` in `lib/tier.ts`) or 2.5 s after load + idle, the floating on the next idle period after it. Layering: one fixed, pointer-events:none canvas (`canvas.liquid`) at `z-index:-1` paints the dark itself, so every section background is transparent and content (text, images, 3D canvases) paints above it. Section effect canvases (the Thesis metal, the footer furnace, the PayPal pour, the ingots) are transparent where there is no heat; some overhang their boxes on purpose, so `main` clips sideways (`overflow-x: clip`, which keeps sticky working). The nav is opaque `--ground` with no `backdrop-filter`: through any translucency the bright 3D under it (Sites bar, logo) showed as a grey box, and a blur over the liquid re-blurs every frame; never make it translucent. Selection: `::selection` is `--ember-tint` (the text keeps its colour); canvases, images, SVG and video are never selectable or draggable (base CSS, plus `draggable={false}` on decorative imgs for Firefox); the home opening `.ig` selects nothing but its paragraph; a mouse drag that starts on the liquid itself (no words under it) selects nothing (`keepDragsForWaves` in `Liquid.tsx`, a `selectstart` guard; keyboard, shift-click, touch and drags from text untouched), since dragging is how visitors make waves. A canvas React renders is never swapped out of the DOM (a worker-to-main fallback puts its fresh canvas next to React's spent one): a swapped node breaks every later client-side navigation (WebKit).
- One clock: `loop.ts` `onFrame(cb, {early})` is the only rAF and the only animation time (it idles in hidden tabs): early callbacks (layout reads) run first, then the liquid (input, grid step), then floaters and scenes. Workers keep no clock: they draw exactly the `t` the main frame posts and ack it (one tick in flight, the newest waits; the ingot worker acks only after a GPU fence on that frame (`afterGPU` in `ingotRunner.ts`), so a slow GPU paces the ticks instead of queueing frames); every renderer registers with `renderer(name, path)` and reports `done(t)`, paused while off screen. - Floating (`lib/liquid/float.ts`: `startFloat`, `LEVELS`): each floater feels the surface under its whole box (`footprint()` in `field.ts`: mean height, best-plane tilt, mean slope) through second-order springs (no dead band, no hard clamps, a soft tanh limit only on large waves), written as CSS `translate`/`rotate` (so it composes with `transform` reveals; layout never changes). Both 3D scenes compute their poses on the main thread every clock tick and post them with it; the renderer (worker or main) applies exactly those. The ingots: `samplePx` + `buoy3`. The logo (`logoScene.ts`): every piece on its own, the story's offsets plus `buoy3` on the surface under it (a square or the core: five points across it, an arc: nine along it; the swell at a quarter of its height so the mark stays readable at rest); the cursor pushes them through the same springs (its waves' energy pushes them away and tips them, the pointer is a soft hill that flattens once it rests; no clamp). There is no second wave model (`field.ts` has no `sample()`/`hooks`). Floating never dims or masks anything behind a floater. Excluded always: nav, header, forms (and ancestors holding one), dialogs, fixed, inline and `display: contents` elements; nothing moves while a form field has focus. Off under reduced motion.

## Rules (from the PRD)
- Never write "LLC", "incorporated", a street address, or any payment details (Venmo etc.). Payment copy: "No payment until you've approved the design."
- No fabricated testimonials, client logos, reviews or stats. Showcase items stay labeled `Concept` until they are real clients.
- Colour means temperature. Ember (`--ember`) is the only accent and means hot (active, interactive, live): about 3 Ember elements per screen at most. `--ember-hot` only inside hot metal. Cooled surfaces flip to bone (`--cool`, `--ink`, `--ink-dim`, `--ember-deep` for Ember text on bone).
- Placeholders are marked `// TODO(jakob)`.
- Quality bar: Lighthouse mobile ≥ 95 on all four categories, `/sites` first-load JS well under 200 KB gzipped, no horizontal scroll at 360px, AA contrast, visible Ember focus rings.
- No gradient text and no second display face: hierarchy comes from Archivo's size, weight and width (`.wide`, `.h-display`, `.h-section`). The one exception is "alloyed" in the home opening, which is cast white-hot and cools (`.ig-cool`).
- Design rules live in `.claude/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`; `npm run unslop` checks what a machine can (headings without periods, no Instrument Serif or mono outside data, no wide-tracked caps, no decorative numbering, no dead zones on the home page).
