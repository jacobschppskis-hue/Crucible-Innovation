# crucibleinnovation.com

Marketing site for Crucible (studio, `/`) and Crucible Sites (websites for local businesses, `/sites`, the page the business-card QR opens). Owner: Jacob Schopp. Read `README.md` for setup, deploy and the launch TODO list. What Jacob likes and dislikes, across every round, is in `plan/final/JACOB.md`: read it before changing how anything looks or moves.

## Stack and commands
- Next.js 15 App Router, **static export** (`output: 'export'` → `out/`), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4. No server: no API routes, no `next/headers`, no runtime redirects, no image optimizer.
- `npm run dev` · `npm run build` · `npm run typecheck` · `npm test` (Playwright against `out/`, so build first; locally set `PW_CHROMIUM_PATH` to a Chromium binary if the pinned browser is missing; run the GL-heavy specs with `--workers=2` on a GPU-less machine) · `npm run world:smoke` (fast check that the home page's world starts and draws) · `npm run world:preview` (after a build: the home page as ONE self-contained file, `world/dist/crucible.html`, that renders inside the claude.ai artifact frame).
- Deploy: push to `main` → `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` builds and publishes to GitHub Pages (custom domain via `CNAME`). PRs run `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (typecheck, build, Playwright, Lighthouse CI with `lighthouserc.json`).
- Repo variables (optional): `UMAMI_WEBSITE_ID` (analytics), `FORMSPREE_ID` (in-page form posting; without it the form composes an email via mail app/Gmail/Outlook).

## Where things live
- Copy and content: `data/*.ts` (`home.ts` the home page's own lines, `story.ts` the founders' accounts (word for word), `site.ts` founder/email/Formspree, `ventures.ts`, `work.ts`, `pricing.ts`, `faq.ts`, `team.ts`). Prefer editing data over components.
- Pages: `app/page.tsx` (the world), `app/sites/page.tsx`, `app/work/page.tsx`, `app/ventures/page.tsx`, `app/ventures/[slug]` (generated for every venture without `href`), `app/not-found.tsx`. `app/layout.tsx` mounts what every page shares (the page liquid, reveals, scroll driver, magnetic lean, Lenis, the route transition, the `?ref=` tracker).

### The home page: one world around a molten planet (`lib/world/`, contract in `lib/world/ARCHITECTURE.md`)
- `app/page.tsx` renders every section as static, readable HTML (server components in `components/world/`); each section is a place on a journey round the planet (`data-place`: hero, together, story, paypal, story-2, story-3, ventures, thesis, shore, dawn, loop). `components/world/WorldMount.tsx` starts `startWorld(root)` after load + idle (+2.5 s) or at first use; on software GL (no GPU: Lighthouse, CI) only at first use, unless the URL has `?world=full`, `?t=`, `?q=`, `?motion` or `?still` (every frame there is a long main-thread task: the idle start made Lighthouse mobile `/` score 58). The headline and nav never wait for it; until it starts, the page shows the hero still (`public/world/hero-{portrait,landscape}.webp`, `lab/world/still.mjs`).
- The engine (`lib/world/engine.ts`) paints one fixed full-screen WebGL2 canvas (`canvas.world`, z -1, pointer-events none) behind the page: scroll → journey (`journey.ts`, keyed places, a critically damped camera) → modules → scene → bloom → post. Planet radius 6000 units (~1 m low over the sea); one lap is one day; the sun is the mark's glowing core.
- Features are modules registered one line each in `lib/world/modules.ts`, each owning its GLSL (`lib/world/glsl/*.glsl`) and uniforms: `look` (sky, thin atmosphere with LUTs, stars, the sun, the orbit planet: temperature across the disk and rivers with a hot thread and cooler banks; tone curve, bloom, a little halation (`?lens=0` off), grain), `lava` (the smooth molten sea: blackbody under a thin skin, deep-water swell filtered by pixel footprint, the visitor's boat wakes in `wake.ts`/`input.ts`), `mark` (Jacob's mark hung round the sun, cast metal lit by its core; `presence()` decides where on the lap it shows: whole through the hero and together, gone in orbit, back out of the sun's glare on the last screen before the loop; `together.ts`: "stronger together", two snaking rivers that join under the mark, which is never taken apart), `paypal` (a white-hot vent throws six sparks; each lands as a spring whose river joins look's own, which `paypal.ts` ports to the CPU as `lookField()`: change the river field in `planet.glsl` and that port together; hairline name labels, step-by-step captions from Jacob's own text), `ventures` (three tall satin-metal castings breaking the surface far off, filmed low on a long lens; links that work by tap), `thesis` (293 K → 1811 K from orbit: one river on a cold world, then the sea heats from its banks), `shore` (the footer's sunrise over the limb, the day-side crossing with the sky's own sun disc, the cape of black sand on the way down).
- **The loop:** the last place (`loop`, an inert clone of the hero's first screen) makes the bottom flow into the top (`lib/world/loop.ts`, Lenis `infinite` on desktop, our own touch scrolling with iOS-like deceleration on phones; keys wrap too). Every module's frame at progress 1 equals its frame at 0. Off with reduced motion, without JS and with `?loop=0`: the page simply ends.
- Quality steps (`quality.ts`: render scale, the canvas never resizes for a step), software GL detection, `?q=<step>`, `?world=full`, `?t=<s>` (fixed clock), `?still`, `?motion`. Test hooks: `window.__world` (`state()`, `go(p)`, `goPlace(name, f)`, `hold()`, `step(ms)`), `html[data-world]`, `html[data-world-on]`, `html[data-world-fail]`, `html[data-loop]`.
- Without the world (no JS, no WebGL2, reduced motion) the page is a designed still (`components/world/World.css`) with all content readable.

### The other pages (/sites, /work, /ventures, 404)
- **The page liquid** (`lib/liquid/`, mounted by `components/Liquid.tsx` in the layout): the same molten sea seen from straight above, under the text. It steps aside on any page with the world (`[data-world-root]` / `html[data-world]`, checked on every route change; each restart gets a fresh canvas node, since a canvas given to a worker is spent). The surface is a damped wave-equation grid on the main thread (`sim.ts`: 8 px cells, 120 Hz substeps, anchored to the page, stepped only where there are waves) plus the pointer's Kelvin-like wake (`wake.ts`: a ~19° V at any speed, no beads, no rings) and an analytic idle swell (`swell.ts`: `swellAt` on the CPU and `swellGLSL` in shaders, the same sum). `shader.ts` shades it with lava's model (lib/world/glsl/lava.glsl): a true blackbody (Planck at 600/550/460 nm) under a thin skin, dimmed by Fresnel; 975 K at rest (dark: text stays AA), a little hotter in the swell's troughs, and hotter along the visitor's waves (brief Ember-to-amber lines); a dark red night mirrored in it, crisp Ember glints on interaction waves only. No noise, no crust, no masks: waves pass under everything. `render.ts` posts heights to `liquidWorker.ts` (OffscreenCanvas); where the worker can't get WebGL2 the same `engine.ts` draws on the main thread. `?liquid=full` pins the top step. It starts at the first scroll, pointer move or key (`firstUse()` in `lib/tier.ts`) or 2.5 s after load + idle.
- **Input** (`input.ts`): hover makes the wake, a drag 1.85x; a resting cursor the page scrolls past leaves a wake along its page path; a finger's wake lives in viewport px; a tap is one clean drop. A mouse drag that starts on the liquid itself selects nothing (`keepDragsForWaves`). `html:not([data-world])` has `overscroll-behavior-x: none`, so a sideways finger drag makes waves instead of swiping back in history (Chrome on Android).
- **Floating** (`lib/liquid/float.ts`): text, headings and blocks ride the surface (`footprint()` in `field.ts`) through second-order springs, written as CSS `translate`/`rotate`; links and buttons ride interaction waves only and then rest exactly, so clicks land. `data-float="strong|heading|block|text|none"`. One writer per element (the magnetic lean `lib/liquid/lean.ts` is added in `float.ts`). Never the nav, forms, dialogs, fixed or inline elements; nothing moves while a form field has focus. Off under reduced motion.
- **One clock:** `lib/liquid/loop.ts` `onFrame(cb, {early})` is the only rAF and the only animation time, for the world too (it idles in hidden tabs). Workers draw exactly the `t` they are posted and ack it. Test hooks: `window.__clock` (`pause`, `step(ms)`, `resume`, `settle(t)`, `renderers`), `window.__liquid`, `window.__float.list()`; the liquid canvas carries `data-on`, `data-t`, `data-live`, `data-q`, `data-path`.
- **Motion:** no animation library. Reveals = `[data-reveal]` / `[data-words]` / `[data-cast]` + one IntersectionObserver (`components/InView.tsx`: hides only what it measured below the viewport, shows anything in view or above at once, `data-snap` after jumps, so nothing on screen ever fades out). Scroll-linked effects use `lib/scroll.ts` (`data-scroll="view|enter|pin|reveal|read"` → CSS `var(--p)`, or `trackScroll()`): an exact critically damped spring per element with no speed cap and no landing snap (never add a "land at once" rule back). Pinned scenes: `BuildStage` (/sites opening, live concept sites in `LiveSite`), `Process` (seven scrubbable days), the /work `Stack`s (one plate at a time; a list on phones, short screens, reduced motion and no JS). /work (h1 "Out of the crucible"; the nav still says Work) leads with live client sites (`data/work.ts` `clientSites`, real ones only): one that can be framed is embedded (`ClientFrame`), one that can't (Norma: `X-Frame-Options`) is a film (`ClientFilm`): real screenshots at moments down its page, in a browser and a phone, that the scroll plays with a one-line caption each (the next moment wipes up from the bottom of the screen: never a dissolve, a scroll that rests halfway showed a double exposure). On wide screens its plate holds the pinned stack for the film (`--m`, `--f`, `filmPlates`); on phones the phone frame pins under the nav (`.wk-reel`, its own `data-scroll="pin"`); reduced motion, no JS and short screens get the top of the page in both frames and the other moments as captioned stills. `BeforeAfter` sweeps once on first view. Lenis smooths wheel/trackpad (`components/SmoothScroll.tsx`), not on touch, not on the world's page.
- **Ventures** (/ventures, /ventures/[slug]): each venture is its metal monolith, a transparent still the home page's own shader renders (`public/film/monoliths/<name>-{600,1200,2400}.webp`, `node scripts/monolith-stills.mjs`: it renders from the world's state at `ventures` f 0.6 stored in `lab/ventures/still.html`; after a change to the home page's castings run it with `--capture` on a fresh build first; `data/ventures.ts` `monolith`), standing on the page liquid with no floor or box; hover lifts it. A venture without a still shows its mark in CSS (`MarkCast`). Share images: `node scripts/venture-og.mjs` → `public/og/<slug>.jpg`.
- **Footer** (`components/Footer.tsx`, the other pages; the home page's shore is `components/world/Footer.tsx`, same links and `id="pages"`, which the no-JS menu link targets): dark, with the furnace under the giant wordmark (`Furnace.tsx`, `FurnaceGL.tsx`, `lib/gl/furnaceGL.ts`: glowing surf Jacob likes; the CSS band is a still copy, the GL fades in over it on the one clock via `--gl`; loops run only on screen). Never the bone footer, never streak-line flames.
- **Nav** (`components/Nav.tsx`, `MobileMenu.tsx`): opaque `--ground`, never translucent and no `backdrop-filter` (a bright thing under a translucent bar shows as a grey box). It fits 360 px phones (`.nav-brand`/`.nav-cta` below 390 px).
- **Founders' faces:** Jacob and Charlie sent two posters they made (Nov 10; the originals are not in the repo). Each chapter on the home page opens on its founder's own poster, cropped so the pair shares no panels (`public/team/poster-<name>-{540,1080}.webp`, `data/story.ts` `poster`; Jacob's keeps the top: contour lines, the eye, the cap and laptop; Charlie's the rest: the math, the 45 lb plate, the code panel, the logo, the mountains). The /sites builders are CRT tubes: `python3 scripts/crt-portraits.py` turns `public/team/src/<name>.jpg` (gitignored: source photos are never committed; today head-and-shoulders crops of the posters) into `public/team/<name>-crt.webp`, a bone-phosphor tube (never an RGB mask); `NO_SIGNAL` in `Builders.tsx` is off now that they show faces (on, a tube shows "No signal" and the name).
- **Images:** showcase screenshots come from `showcase-src/*.html` via `showcase-src/render.mjs` (also the `-640`/`-960` variants `lib/imageLoader.ts` serves). A client site's come from `node scripts/shoot-client.mjs <slug> <url> [<key>=<selector>@<fraction> ...]` (the exact command is beside its entry in `data/work.ts`): the top of the page plus one shot per moment, desktop (1440, `-960`, `-640`) and phone (780 with the status bar, `-390`), all lazy and sized, about 0.25 MB a phone visit. The /work business card is `brand/business-card-{front,back}.png` (`node brand/render-card.mjs`). The "This site" shots (`public/work/crucible-site*.webp`) come from `node scripts/shoot-own.mjs` on the built `out/` (the world's hero at `?q=3` with the clock running: a held clock never presents a frame for a screenshot, and `?world=full` takes minutes a frame under software GL; ~2 min): re-run it after home-page visual changes.

### Brand
- Tokens and shared CSS in `app/globals.css`; component CSS is imported by the component/page that uses it (only globals and `Sites.css` are global).
- The mark is Jacob's logo family (`lib/splitRing.ts`, `components/SplitRing.tsx`): rings r 20 and 12 on his 48-unit grid (×4/3 in a 64 box), stroke 2.6, upright squares 3.2 centred on the circle (7 on the outer ring 22.5° apart, 4 on the inner 36° apart) after one empty step, core 3.6; each ring's left half an exact half circle ending on the core's centre line. Never a lattice, never a square welded to a band, never taken apart. `tests/mark.spec.ts` enforces it (2D and the extrusion in `lib/gl3d/geometry.ts`). Small sizes use the brand mark (one ring, four squares, the core; `brandMarkSvg()`): the favicon is its pixel-snapped 16 px master. Static copies come from `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/mark-assets.mjs`, then `node brand/render-og.mjs` and `node brand/render-card.mjs`. `components/Wordmark.tsx` (dotless ı + Ember dot).
- Fonts: Archivo only, self-hosted as one variable file (`app/fonts/`, wght 400-800, wdth 100-125, Latin subset; recipe in `README.md`). The concept sites' Instrument Serif loads after load + idle (`html[data-fonts]`, `releaseConceptFonts` in `lib/tier.ts`).
- Tiers (`lib/tier.ts`): every visit gets the rich version, loaded after load + idle (Jacob's call: no low-bandwidth version). Light (CSS/SVG, stills) is only for reduced motion, no JS, and a scene that can't start. `?tier=rich|light` forces one. Every rich effect needs that light version and lowers its own quality on slow frames rather than stall.
- Print: `brand/business-card.html` → `node brand/render-card.mjs` → `brand/business-card.pdf`.

## Rules (from the PRD and Jacob's reviews)
- Never write "LLC", "incorporated", a street address, or any payment details (Venmo etc.). Payment copy: "No payment until you've approved the design."
- No fabricated testimonials, client logos, reviews or stats. Showcase items stay labeled `Concept` until they are real clients. Every fact on the home page comes from Jacob's own text; the founders' accounts stay word for word; his sentence "I want to know what happens when a group like it comes together earlier." appears once.
- Colour means temperature. Ember (`--ember`) is the only hot accent (active, interactive, live): about 3 Ember elements per screen at most. `--ember-hot` only inside hot metal. Quench (`--quench` #8DB6F2) only for cold things (stars, the cool sky band, a cold readout), about 5% of a screen at most, never on UI or the mark. Cooled surfaces are bone (`--bone`, `--cool`, `--ink`, `--ink-dim`, `--ember-deep` for Ember text on bone).
- No masks, scrims, halos or boxes behind text: legibility comes from composition and a dark sea (the page liquid's rest background stays under ~0.01 relative luminance).
- Clean lava: smooth, glossy, water-like molten metal. No dark crust plates, specks, mottling, flaking or grime anywhere.
- Film, not a video game: physically motivated light, a proper tone curve, no clipped hues, no plastic specular, no banding, no visible tiling.
- Placeholders are marked `// TODO(jakob)`.
- Quality bar: Lighthouse mobile ≥ 95 on all four categories, `/sites` first-load JS well under 200 KB gzipped, no horizontal scroll at 360px, AA contrast, visible Ember focus rings, and mobile (iPhone) is the main target.
- No gradient text and no second display face: hierarchy comes from Archivo's size, weight and width (`.wide`, `.h-display`, `.h-section`).
- Design rules live in `.claude/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`; `npm run unslop` checks what a machine can (headings without periods, no Instrument Serif or mono outside data, no wide-tracked caps, no decorative numbering, the world answering the scroll at every position).
