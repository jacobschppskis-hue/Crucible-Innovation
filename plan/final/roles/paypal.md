# Role: paypal (PayPal → six companies)

Jacob chose "six sparks across the planet", and warned: "you better execute it well. You have to find a way that makes it
actually work, because this is very easy for you to mess up. It ends up looking very dumb, it doesn't fit with the
website, and it doesn't fit with the look of the planet. So just find a way to do it properly. Ways that you can
incorporate different objects into the transition, ways that you can make the spreading [...] fit better with the
aesthetic of the website."

## Deliver
- A beat at cosmic scale (the planet from orbit or high altitude, night side, where light reads best): PayPal as one hot
  source on the planet; six embers leave it and travel across the planet's curvature (great-circle arcs, real
  perspective, motion that obeys something physical: ballistic arcs, a molten droplet splitting, light trails that cool
  and fade, nothing left sitting), and each lands and lights a place that becomes a company: YouTube, LinkedIn, Yelp,
  Palantir (founded by PayPal's people), SpaceX and Tesla (Elon funded with his share). The names sit in the world
  (anchored to their landing places), legible on a phone, Archivo, no boxes behind them.
- Step-by-step captions from Jacob's own text (`data/story.ts`), timed to the animation, e.g. eBay buys PayPal in 2002 →
  members of that group went on to found YouTube, LinkedIn, Yelp and Palantir → Elon used his share to fund SpaceX and
  later invest in Tesla → "so much came out of a single company". No facts that are not in his text.
- Scroll-scrubbed, reversible, continuous; looks right at every frame on iPhone 15 first. Storyboard frames + a short
  plan to the coordinator within the first hour, then build.
