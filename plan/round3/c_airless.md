# Your concept: "Airless" (a molten ocean on a planet with no atmosphere)

Branch: `claude/inspiring-allen-sz5kjl-concept-airless`. Slug: `airless`.

Jacob's own idea for this one (verbatim): "Another idea for the one that has the skyline, the one where it has the
horizon as the molten lava ocean, and it has the sun as the nucleus of the Crucible logo. Make it so that the sky is kind
of lit up by it, but it still looks like a world without an atmosphere or something. You can have stars in the sky or
something. I think that'd be cool. Really develop that so it looks like a planet without an atmosphere. You can have the
sand in the background for some of them, and then it goes back to the ocean, so it looks like it's going around the
planet for it. You still have creative control though, but just develop one off of that."

Direction (develop it; beat it if you can):
- Hero: eye-level over a molten lava ocean to a curved horizon (the planet's curvature visible). The logo's core is the
  sun, hovering just above the sea (like the reference: the subject floating over water), its reflection a molten column
  on the swell, concentric ripples spreading under it. The rest of the mark lives in the scene: e.g. the two rings as a
  vast thin halo around the sun (the reference has a thin ring around the subject), the machine-half squares as a ring of
  satellites/debris catching light, or the mark drawn by light on the sea. No air: a pure black sky, crisp stars that
  don't twinkle, no haze, no blue sky gradient, a sharp sun disc with a corona and zodiacal-light glow (that's the "light
  colour in the sky"), maybe a cold blue (Quench) planet or moon low in the sky as the counterweight to the sun; shadows
  are hard; distant black-sand islands or dunes on the horizon. Black-sand shore in the foreground with molten surf
  lapping constantly (the footer motif, now at the top). The cursor/finger makes boat-like wakes in the lava.
- Scroll is a journey around the planet: the camera travels from the shore out over the ocean, over sand again, back to
  ocean, the sun moving across the sky, each section of the page a place on the way. Make the later sections live in
  that world (the founders' story, the PayPal moment, the ventures as things rising from or floating on the sea, the
  thesis, the footer surf).
- Type: e.g. headlines with a real reflection in the lava below them; small labels like a star chart in Quench.
