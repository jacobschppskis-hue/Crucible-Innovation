# Your concept: "Abyss" (top-down over the molten sea)

Branch: `claude/inspiring-allen-sz5kjl-concept-abyss`. Slug: `abyss`.

Jacob asked for concepts "that just go top-down on the ocean". Direction (develop it; beat it if you can):
- The camera looks straight down at a molten sea that fills the whole page and scrolls with it (the page is the ocean
  surface). Crust plates drifting on currents, glowing seams between them, slow convection, caustic light, a constant
  gentle swell; the cursor/finger cuts boat-like wakes through it (smooth V lines; a drag breaks the crust more).
- Hero: the Split Ring floating on the sea seen from above: the core is the sun-bright nucleus reflected from above /
  glowing up through the surface, ripples spreading from it; the arcs and squares are cast pieces floating on the
  surface, bobbing, pushing ripples. As you scroll, the sea moves under you (drift, currents) and things surface and
  sink.
- Ventures: "a subject comes out of the lava": each venture surfaces from the molten sea as you reach it (lava drains
  off it, it cools from orange to bone as it rises, the ripples close behind it), e.g. Crucible Sites a cooled cast bar
  with the mark, Verge and Trellis still molten.
- Type: e.g. headlines as cooled metal plates floating on the lava with glowing seams at their edges, still perfectly
  readable; Quench for the coolant: the cool sheen on cooled crust, a light from above.
